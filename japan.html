Suica, without fighting it from India

Try this from Bengaluru

  • Wallet → Transit Card → Suica or PASMO.
  • Eligible payment card already in Wallet. Mastercard / Amex usually fail less than foreign Visa.
  • Turn on Express Mode. Tap the top of the iPhone on the gate. No unlock.
  • If issuance or top-up is blocked outside Japan, stop. Do not burn an evening on it.

Finish on airport Wi-Fi

  • Apple’s own page (updated 25 Mar 2026) covers add / transfer / Express Mode.
  • Welcome Suica Mobile is a fallback if Wallet refuses a foreign card.
  • Cash top-up at JR machines still exists if Apple Pay sulks.
  • You only need one IC card. Suica and PASMO are interchangeable on the gates.
