Goodbyes · omiyage · distance

Leave as a person, not a logistics problem.

The last Bengaluru week will try to become fourteen guilt-meetings. Pick the few that matter. Pack modest gifts. Set the channel you will actually keep from Tokyo.

Who gets time

  • Family: one intentional meal or day. Quality over duration.
  • Two or three friends you do not want distance to quietly delete.
  • One ordinary Bengaluru routine you like — gym, coffee, walk — not a farewell tour.
  • Everyone else: a message is enough. You are not a departing dignitary.

Omiyage rules

  • Individually wrapped, shelf-stable, small.
  • Tea, chocolate, snacks. No homemade food. No meat. No fresh sweets.
  • Japan Animal Quarantine is not joking about animal products.
  • This is a gesture for coworkers / new friends, not cargo.
