What this page is really about

Do now

  • International POS / ATM / online on the primary card. Check limits.
  • Second card that can float a week if the first is frozen.
  • Download statements. Kill India-only autopays.
  • Screenshot HDFC resident→NRO form and KYC list.

Do after you are actually NRI

  • Redesignate accounts to NRO. HDFC will typically hotlist the international debit card and issue a replacement.
  • You will need PAN, passport, visa, and an overseas address.
  • Map FDs, demat, SIPs, joint accounts before you touch anything.
  • Company arrival cash is excluded. This is your own float and India cleanup.
